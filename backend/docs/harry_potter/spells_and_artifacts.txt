TITLE: Harry Potter — Spells, Magical Artifacts, and the Deathly Hallows
SERIES: Harry Potter
COVERAGE: Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone through Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows
SPOILER LEVEL: FULL
CANON BASIS: J.K. Rowling's seven original Harry Potter novels

OVERVIEW

Magic in the Harry Potter universe is performed through spells, potions, enchanted objects, magical creatures, and other supernatural practices.

Most witches and wizards use wands to focus their abilities, although magic is not entirely dependent on wands.

Magical results depend on factors such as skill, knowledge, intent, and the properties of the spell being performed.

While many spells are used for ordinary tasks, others are designed for combat, deception, healing, or manipulation.

Several particularly important magical artifacts, including Horcruxes and the Deathly Hallows, are central to Voldemort's pursuit of immortality and Harry Potter's eventual victory.

COMMON SPELLS AND CHARMS

EXPELLIARMUS — DISARMING CHARM

Expelliarmus causes a target to lose possession of an object, particularly a wand.

Harry uses the Disarming Charm repeatedly and becomes strongly associated with it.

Although it is less destructive than many offensive spells, its use can prove decisive in magical confrontations.

STUPEFY — STUNNING SPELL

Stupefy renders a target unconscious or otherwise temporarily incapacitated.

It is commonly used by Aurors and other witches and wizards during combat.

PROTEGO — SHIELD CHARM

Protego creates a magical barrier capable of deflecting or blocking many spells.

Its effectiveness depends on the attack and the caster's ability.

Ordinary Shield Charms do not reliably stop the Killing Curse.

ACCIO — SUMMONING CHARM

Accio draws an object toward the caster.

Harry uses this spell during the Triwizard Tournament to summon his broomstick.

WINGARDIUM LEVIOSA — LEVITATION CHARM

Wingardium Leviosa allows the caster to levitate objects.

It is one of the first practical spells taught to Hogwarts students.

ALOHOMORA — UNLOCKING CHARM

Alohomora opens many locked doors and other mechanisms.

Some locks are protected by enchantments that resist the spell.

LUMOS AND NOX

Lumos produces light from the tip of a wand.

Nox extinguishes light created by the wand-lighting spell.

PETRIFICUS TOTALUS — FULL BODY-BIND CURSE

Petrificus Totalus temporarily immobilizes the victim's body.

Hermione uses this spell against Neville Longbottom in the first novel.

OBLIVIATE — MEMORY CHARM

Obliviate is associated with removing or altering memories.

Memory modification is used by wizarding authorities to maintain secrecy and by other characters for manipulation or concealment.

Poorly performed or forceful memory modification can have serious consequences.

EXPECTO PATRONUM — PATRONUS CHARM

Expecto Patronum conjures a magical protective force especially effective against Dementors.

A fully formed Patronus commonly takes the shape of an animal.

The spell often requires the caster to concentrate on strong positive emotions or memories.

Harry's Patronus takes the form of a stag, resembling the animal associated with his father.

Some members of the Order of the Phoenix also use Patronuses to carry messages.

RIDDIKULUS

Riddikulus is used against Boggarts, shapeshifting creatures that assume forms representing a person's fears.

The spell encourages a transformation that makes the frightening appearance humorous.

THE UNFORGIVABLE CURSES

The Unforgivable Curses are three powerful forms of Dark magic whose use against another human being is severely prohibited under wizarding law.

They are associated with coercion, torture, and murder.

AVADA KEDAVRA — THE KILLING CURSE

Avada Kedavra normally causes instantaneous death when successfully cast against a living target.

It is characterized by a flash of green light.

Ordinary defensive spells cannot simply block it in the manner of less powerful curses.

However, unusual magical circumstances, physical obstacles, and sacrificial protection can interfere with its intended effect.

Voldemort uses Avada Kedavra against Lily and James Potter, Harry Potter, and many other victims.

The curse also becomes central to Voldemort's own defeat when his final attack rebounds.

CRUCIO — THE CRUCIATUS CURSE

Crucio inflicts severe pain upon its victim.

Death Eaters use the curse for torture, intimidation, and punishment.

Frank and Alice Longbottom, Neville's parents, are tortured into permanent incapacitation by Death Eaters including Bellatrix Lestrange.

The curse requires a genuine intention to cause suffering to achieve its full effect.

IMPERIO — THE IMPERIUS CURSE

Imperio allows the caster to exert control over another person's behavior.

Victims can be compelled to perform actions against their own wishes.

Some individuals are capable of resisting its influence.

The curse is frequently used by Voldemort's followers to manipulate officials, conceal conspiracies, and seize political authority.

OTHER IMPORTANT DARK MAGIC

Sectumsempra is a dangerous cutting curse created by Severus Snape during his student years.

Harry discovers it in Snape's old Potions textbook and uses it against Draco Malfoy without understanding its effects.

The spell causes deep wounds and can be fatal.

Fiendfyre is extraordinarily destructive enchanted fire that can take the form of monstrous creatures.

It is difficult to control and capable of destroying objects protected by extremely powerful Dark magic, including Horcruxes.

Vincent Crabbe creates Fiendfyre inside the Room of Requirement during the final novel. The fire destroys Rowena Ravenclaw's diadem and kills Crabbe.

HORCRUXES

A Horcrux is an object or living being containing a fragment of a wizard's soul.

Creating a Horcrux requires the soul to be damaged through murder, followed by additional Dark magic.

The complete ritual is not described in detail in the novels.

The purpose of a Horcrux is to anchor its creator to life, preventing ordinary death from permanently eliminating the creator while soul fragments remain secured elsewhere.

Horcruxes are among the darkest forms of magic in the wizarding world.

Voldemort seeks to make his soul exceptionally difficult to destroy by dividing it into multiple pieces.

He originally intends to create six Horcruxes, leaving a seventh portion of his soul within his own body.

However, when his Killing Curse rebounds from infant Harry, an additional fragment attaches itself to Harry unintentionally.

As a result, Harry becomes an accidental Horcrux without Voldemort knowing.

VOLDEMORT'S HORCRUXES

TOM RIDDLE'S DIARY

The diary contains a fragment of Voldemort's soul created during his youth.

It manipulates Ginny Weasley into opening the Chamber of Secrets and releasing the Basilisk.

Harry destroys the diary with a Basilisk fang in the Chamber of Secrets.

MARVOLO GAUNT'S RING

The ring is an heirloom connected to Voldemort's maternal family.

It contains a soul fragment and incorporates the Resurrection Stone, one of the Deathly Hallows.

Dumbledore destroys the Horcrux using the Sword of Gryffindor.

However, he first makes the mistake of putting on the cursed ring, suffering a magical injury that eventually contributes to his death.

SALAZAR SLYTHERIN'S LOCKET

The locket is an ancient heirloom of Slytherin that Voldemort transforms into a Horcrux.

Regulus Black learns that Voldemort has hidden something valuable in a cave and attempts to replace the locket with a duplicate.

The real locket eventually passes through several hands.

Harry, Ron, and Hermione recover it during their search for Horcruxes.

Ron destroys the locket using the Sword of Gryffindor after overcoming its psychological influence.

HELGA HUFFLEPUFF'S CUP

The cup is a valuable artifact belonging to one of Hogwarts' founders.

Voldemort steals it after murdering Hepzibah Smith and later transforms it into a Horcrux.

It is eventually stored in Bellatrix Lestrange's vault at Gringotts.

Harry, Ron, and Hermione steal the cup from the vault.

Hermione destroys it using a Basilisk fang obtained from the Chamber of Secrets.

ROWENA RAVENCLAW'S DIADEM

The diadem belonged to Hogwarts founder Rowena Ravenclaw and was associated with enhancing wisdom.

It was stolen by her daughter Helena, who later became the ghost known as the Grey Lady.

Voldemort discovers the hidden diadem and transforms it into a Horcrux.

He conceals it in the Room of Requirement at Hogwarts.

The diadem is destroyed by Fiendfyre during Harry's confrontation with Draco Malfoy, Crabbe, and Goyle.

NAGINI

Nagini is Voldemort's enormous serpent and a particularly trusted companion.

Voldemort turns her into a Horcrux after returning to power in a weakened state.

Nagini participates in multiple attacks and is used to kill Severus Snape.

During the Battle of Hogwarts, Neville Longbottom kills Nagini using the Sword of Gryffindor.

HARRY POTTER — ACCIDENTAL HORCRUX

Harry carries a fragment of Voldemort's soul without knowing it.

This connection helps explain Harry's ability to speak Parseltongue and his recurring visions involving Voldemort.

When Harry voluntarily confronts Voldemort in the Forbidden Forest, Voldemort casts the Killing Curse against him.

The curse destroys the soul fragment residing inside Harry.

Harry survives because of extraordinary circumstances involving Lily's sacrificial protection and Voldemort's use of Harry's blood during his resurrection.

After this encounter, Harry is no longer a Horcrux.

DESTROYING A HORCRUX

A Horcrux cannot normally be destroyed through ordinary damage.

Its container must be damaged beyond magical repair so that it can no longer sustain the soul fragment.

Basilisk venom can destroy Horcruxes because it causes exceptionally powerful damage.

The Sword of Gryffindor acquires the destructive properties of Basilisk venom after Harry uses it to kill the Basilisk.

Fiendfyre is another destructive force powerful enough to destroy Horcruxes.

Destroying a Horcrux does not automatically destroy every other Horcrux. Each container must be dealt with individually.

The destruction of all Horcruxes allows Voldemort to be killed permanently.

THE DEATHLY HALLOWS

The Deathly Hallows are three legendary magical objects associated with the Peverell brothers.

According to the story known as The Tale of the Three Brothers, Death gave the brothers three extraordinary gifts.

The tale is a legend, however, and the objects have a history that can be understood without treating Death's appearance as a proven historical event.

THE ELDER WAND

The Elder Wand is an exceptionally powerful wand historically associated with Antioch Peverell.

Its ownership depends on magical allegiance rather than ordinary physical possession.

Defeating the wand's previous master may transfer its loyalty to the victor.

Dumbledore becomes the Elder Wand's master after defeating Grindelwald.

Draco Malfoy later disarms Dumbledore on the Astronomy Tower, unknowingly gaining its allegiance.

Harry subsequently defeats Draco and takes his wand at Malfoy Manor, transferring allegiance to Harry.

Voldemort steals the Elder Wand from Dumbledore's tomb but never becomes its true master.

During the final duel, the Elder Wand refuses to defeat its rightful master, Harry, and Voldemort's Killing Curse rebounds.

In the novel, Harry uses the Elder Wand to repair his original wand and intends to return it to Dumbledore's tomb.

THE RESURRECTION STONE

The Resurrection Stone is associated with Cadmus Peverell.

It allows the holder to summon manifestations of deceased individuals, but it does not restore them fully to ordinary physical life.

The stone is hidden inside Marvolo Gaunt's ring.

After removing the ring's curse and destroying the Horcrux, Dumbledore places the stone inside a Golden Snitch and leaves it to Harry.

Before facing Voldemort in the Forbidden Forest, Harry uses the stone to summon the comforting presences of his deceased parents, Sirius Black, and Remus Lupin.

Harry drops the stone in the forest and does not attempt to reclaim it.

THE CLOAK OF INVISIBILITY

The Cloak of Invisibility is associated with Ignotus Peverell.

Unlike ordinary invisibility cloaks, it retains its powers over time and provides exceptionally reliable concealment.

The cloak passes through Harry's family and is given to him during his first year at Hogwarts.

Harry uses it throughout the series to explore Hogwarts, evade enemies, and conduct investigations.

The cloak is extremely powerful but does not guarantee absolute protection against every magical detection method.

OTHER IMPORTANT MAGICAL ARTIFACTS

THE SORTING HAT

The Sorting Hat was originally associated with Godric Gryffindor.

It assigns Hogwarts students to houses based on their qualities and potential.

The hat can also produce the Sword of Gryffindor for a worthy Gryffindor under exceptional circumstances.

THE SWORD OF GRYFFINDOR

The Sword of Gryffindor is a goblin-made blade associated with Godric Gryffindor.

Its enchanted properties allow it to absorb substances that strengthen it, including Basilisk venom.

The sword is used to destroy several Horcruxes and to kill Nagini.

THE MARAUDER'S MAP

The Marauder's Map was created by James Potter, Sirius Black, Remus Lupin, and Peter Pettigrew.

It displays Hogwarts' layout and the locations of many individuals within the school.

Harry acquires the map from Fred and George Weasley.

The map helps him travel through Hogwarts and discover hidden information.

THE PENSIEVE

A Pensieve is a magical vessel used to examine memories.

It allows individuals to observe remembered events from an unusual external perspective.

Dumbledore uses a Pensieve to investigate Voldemort's past and to help Harry understand the creation of Horcruxes.

Memories can be altered or deliberately concealed, making the reliability of the source important.

THE MIRROR OF ERISED

The Mirror of Erised shows a person an image of what they most deeply desire.

Harry initially sees himself surrounded by his deceased family.

Dumbledore uses the mirror as part of the magical protections surrounding the Philosopher's Stone.

THE PHILOSOPHER'S STONE

The Philosopher's Stone was created by Nicolas Flamel.

It can produce the Elixir of Life, which prolongs life when consumed, and can transform metals into gold.

Voldemort attempts to obtain the stone during Harry's first year at Hogwarts.

The stone is eventually destroyed by agreement between Flamel and Dumbledore.

THE GOLDEN SNITCH

The Golden Snitch is a small, fast-moving ball used in Quidditch.

A Seeker earns 150 points by catching it, normally ending a match.

Dumbledore later uses the first Snitch Harry caught to conceal the Resurrection Stone.

THE TIME-TURNER

A Time-Turner is a magical device capable of allowing travel into the past under particular conditions.

Hermione uses one during Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban to attend overlapping classes.

Harry and Hermione later use it to rescue Sirius Black and Buckbeak.

The novels depict significant limitations and dangers associated with time travel.

WANDLORE AND MAGICAL ALLEGIANCE

Wands are specialized magical instruments that help channel a wizard's power.

They vary in wood, core, length, and flexibility.

Common wand cores include phoenix feather, dragon heartstring, and unicorn hair.

A wand may develop a particularly strong relationship with a specific owner.

Harry's original wand and Voldemort's original wand contain feathers from the same phoenix, Fawkes.

This connection produces unusual magical effects when the two wands confront each other.

Wand ownership and magical allegiance are not always determined by who physically holds an instrument.

The Elder Wand's changing allegiance is essential to understanding Voldemort's final defeat.

THE FINAL DEFEAT OF VOLDEMORT

Voldemort believes that possession of the Elder Wand will make him unbeatable.

However, he misunderstands its history and kills Snape under the false assumption that doing so will give him control over the wand.

In reality, Draco Malfoy became the wand's master by disarming Dumbledore, and Harry later became its master by defeating Draco.

By the final confrontation, all of Voldemort's Horcruxes have been destroyed.

Harry casts Expelliarmus while Voldemort casts Avada Kedavra.

Because the Elder Wand is loyal to Harry, Voldemort's Killing Curse rebounds and kills Voldemort permanently.

The outcome demonstrates the combined importance of Horcrux destruction, wand allegiance, and Voldemort's failure to understand forms of magic that cannot be controlled through domination alone.

CANON NOTES AND LIMITATIONS

The seven novels establish the primary magical rules summarized in this document.

Some unusual magical interactions are intentionally mysterious and are not explained through complete technical formulas.

The creation of Horcruxes involves dangerous Dark magic, but the books do not provide a complete procedure.

The Deathly Hallows are distinct from Horcruxes. Horcruxes anchor a damaged soul to life, while the Hallows are extraordinary magical artifacts with different purposes.

Harry does not physically destroy the Elder Wand in the final novel. That action occurs in the film adaptation, not the book.

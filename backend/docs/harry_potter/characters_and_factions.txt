TITLE: Harry Potter — Characters and Factions
SERIES: Harry Potter
COVERAGE: Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone through Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows
SPOILER LEVEL: FULL
CANON BASIS: J.K. Rowling's seven original Harry Potter novels

OVERVIEW

The Harry Potter series centers on Harry Potter and the people who help or oppose him during the struggle against Lord Voldemort.

The conflict involves students, teachers, government officials, resistance organizations, and powerful Dark wizards. Many characters demonstrate that courage, loyalty, ambition, and morality do not always correspond with inherited social status or Hogwarts house membership.

The characters' relationships are deeply affected by grief, friendship, prejudice, secrecy, sacrifice, and the pursuit of power.

HARRY POTTER

Harry James Potter is the son of James and Lily Potter, who were members of the original Order of the Phoenix.

When Harry is an infant, Voldemort attempts to murder him after hearing part of a prophecy concerning a child who could threaten his power.

Lily refuses to stand aside and allow Voldemort to kill Harry. Her willing sacrifice provides Harry with magical protection.

Voldemort's Killing Curse rebounds, destroying his physical body but failing to eliminate his remaining soul fragments.

Harry survives with a lightning-shaped scar on his forehead and becomes known as the Boy Who Lived.

After his parents' deaths, Harry grows up with his aunt Petunia, uncle Vernon, and cousin Dudley Dursley, who treat him poorly.

At eleven, Harry discovers that he is a wizard and begins attending Hogwarts.

He becomes a talented practical defender against Dark magic, a skilled Quidditch Seeker, and a central leader of the resistance against Voldemort.

Harry eventually discovers that he unintentionally carries a fragment of Voldemort's soul, making him an accidental Horcrux.

During the Battle of Hogwarts, Harry willingly faces Voldemort in the Forbidden Forest. The Killing Curse destroys the soul fragment within Harry, but Harry himself survives.

Harry later defeats Voldemort in their final confrontation.

In the epilogue, Harry is married to Ginny Weasley, and they have three children: James Sirius, Albus Severus, and Lily Luna.

HERMIONE GRANGER

Hermione Jean Granger is a Muggle-born witch and one of Harry's closest friends.

She is academically gifted, highly disciplined, and especially skilled at research and learning advanced magic.

Hermione frequently helps Harry and Ron solve mysteries by interpreting books, historical information, and magical clues.

She helps establish Dumbledore's Army and contributes significantly to its organization.

Hermione also campaigns for improved treatment of house-elves, founding the Society for the Promotion of Elfish Welfare, commonly called S.P.E.W.

During the hunt for Voldemort's Horcruxes, Hermione provides essential planning, protective magic, and research.

She helps destroy Hufflepuff's cup using a Basilisk fang recovered from the Chamber of Secrets.

Hermione eventually marries Ron Weasley, and they have two children, Rose and Hugo.

RON WEASLEY

Ronald Bilius Weasley is a member of a large wizarding family known for its opposition to Voldemort and rejection of blood-purity prejudice.

Ron becomes Harry's first close friend at Hogwarts.

He is especially skilled at wizard chess and demonstrates loyalty, courage, and practical thinking.

Although Ron occasionally struggles with jealousy and feelings of inadequacy, he repeatedly risks his life to protect his friends.

During the Horcrux hunt, Ron temporarily leaves Harry and Hermione because of escalating tensions, fear, and the influence of Slytherin's locket.

He later returns, saves Harry from drowning, and destroys the locket Horcrux using the Sword of Gryffindor.

Ron eventually marries Hermione Granger.

ALBUS DUMBLEDORE

Albus Percival Wulfric Brian Dumbledore is the headmaster of Hogwarts for much of the series.

He is an exceptionally powerful wizard and a longtime opponent of Voldemort.

In his youth, Dumbledore develops a close relationship with Gellert Grindelwald, with whom he briefly shares ambitions involving wizarding power and control over Muggles.

A confrontation involving Dumbledore, Grindelwald, and Dumbledore's brother Aberforth results in the death of his sister Ariana. The exact spell responsible for her death remains unknown.

Dumbledore later defeats Grindelwald and becomes the master of the Elder Wand.

He understands that Voldemort has used Horcruxes to survive and arranges for Harry to learn the information necessary to destroy them.

After putting on Marvolo Gaunt's cursed ring, Dumbledore suffers a fatal magical injury that Snape can only temporarily contain.

Knowing that he has limited time left, Dumbledore arranges for Snape to kill him when necessary, partly to spare Draco Malfoy from carrying out Voldemort's command.

Snape kills Dumbledore on the Astronomy Tower in Harry Potter and the Half-Blood Prince.

Dumbledore's plans continue to influence events after his death through information he leaves to Harry, Hermione, Ron, and Snape.

SEVERUS SNAPE

Severus Snape is the Potions professor at Hogwarts and later becomes the Defense Against the Dark Arts teacher and headmaster.

He is a former Death Eater who once supported Voldemort.

Snape loved Lily Evans, Harry's mother, from childhood.

Their friendship deteriorated because of Snape's attraction to Dark magic, his association with future Death Eaters, and his use of a deeply insulting slur against Lily.

When Snape learns that Voldemort intends to kill Lily, he seeks Dumbledore's help to protect her.

After Lily's murder, Snape becomes a double agent working against Voldemort while maintaining the appearance of loyalty to him.

Snape frequently mistreats Harry and other students, despite secretly helping protect Harry.

He kills Dumbledore as part of their prior agreement, although most characters initially interpret the killing as proof of his allegiance to Voldemort.

During the final book, Voldemort orders Nagini to kill Snape because Voldemort incorrectly believes Snape is the master of the Elder Wand.

Before dying, Snape gives Harry memories that reveal his long-term allegiance to Dumbledore, his love for Lily, and the truth about Harry's connection to Voldemort.

Snape's actions are central to Voldemort's eventual defeat, although his personal cruelty remains part of his character.

LORD VOLDEMORT — TOM RIDDLE

Lord Voldemort was born Tom Marvolo Riddle.

His mother, Merope Gaunt, descended from Salazar Slytherin. His father, Tom Riddle Sr., was a Muggle.

Voldemort's ancestry makes him a half-blood wizard despite his later promotion of pure-blood supremacy.

As a student at Hogwarts, Riddle becomes fascinated with immortality, forbidden magic, and the history of Slytherin.

He opens the Chamber of Secrets and murders Myrtle Warren through the Basilisk.

Riddle eventually creates multiple Horcruxes, dividing his soul to protect himself from ordinary death.

He becomes a powerful Dark wizard who commands the Death Eaters and pursues control of the wizarding world.

His first attempt to kill Harry destroys his body. He survives in a weakened state because his soul remains anchored by Horcruxes.

In Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire, Voldemort regains a physical body through a dark ritual involving Harry's blood.

He later takes control of the Ministry of Magic and attempts to eliminate Harry and all organized resistance.

Voldemort's inability to understand love, voluntary sacrifice, and loyalty contributes to his defeat.

His final Killing Curse rebounds because the Elder Wand's allegiance belongs to Harry rather than Voldemort.

DRACO MALFOY

Draco Malfoy is a Slytherin student from an influential pure-blood family.

He initially supports his family's prejudiced beliefs and frequently antagonizes Harry and his friends.

His father, Lucius Malfoy, is a Death Eater and an important supporter of Voldemort.

During Harry Potter and the Half-Blood Prince, Voldemort orders Draco to kill Dumbledore.

Draco becomes increasingly frightened by the consequences of his mission. He disarms Dumbledore but cannot bring himself to kill him.

This disarming is important because it transfers the Elder Wand's allegiance to Draco.

Harry later defeats Draco and takes his wand at Malfoy Manor, unintentionally acquiring the Elder Wand's allegiance.

Draco survives the final war, as do his parents.

THE WEASLEY FAMILY

Arthur and Molly Weasley are members of a large wizarding family that strongly opposes Voldemort's ideology.

Their children include Bill, Charlie, Percy, Fred, George, Ron, and Ginny.

Ginny Weasley becomes an important member of Dumbledore's Army and develops a romantic relationship with Harry.

Fred and George are twins known for magical inventions, pranks, and their joke shop.

Fred is killed during the Battle of Hogwarts.

Molly Weasley kills Bellatrix Lestrange during the final battle while defending her family.

The Weasleys frequently provide Harry with emotional support and a sense of family that he lacked during childhood.

SIRIUS BLACK, REMUS LUPIN, AND PETER PETTIGREW

Sirius Black, Remus Lupin, Peter Pettigrew, and James Potter attended Hogwarts together and were known as the Marauders.

They created the Marauder's Map, which reveals movement and locations within Hogwarts.

Sirius is Harry's godfather. He is wrongly imprisoned in Azkaban for crimes committed by Pettigrew.

Pettigrew secretly betrayed James and Lily Potter to Voldemort. He framed Sirius and escaped by transforming into a rat.

Remus Lupin is a werewolf who becomes one of Harry's most effective Defense Against the Dark Arts teachers.

Sirius is killed by Bellatrix Lestrange during the confrontation at the Department of Mysteries.

Lupin later marries Nymphadora Tonks. Both are killed during the Battle of Hogwarts, leaving behind their infant son Teddy.

Pettigrew dies when the magical silver hand given to him by Voldemort turns against him after he hesitates to harm Harry.

NEVILLE LONGBOTTOM

Neville Longbottom is a Gryffindor student whose parents were tortured into permanent incapacitation by Death Eaters.

He begins the series as an insecure student but gradually develops exceptional courage.

Neville becomes important to Dumbledore's Army and leads student resistance while Hogwarts is controlled by Voldemort's followers.

During the Battle of Hogwarts, Neville draws the Sword of Gryffindor from the Sorting Hat and kills Nagini.

This destroys Voldemort's final remaining Horcrux outside Harry's already-destroyed soul fragment.

LUNA LOVEGOOD

Luna Lovegood is a Ravenclaw student known for her unusual beliefs, independence, and kindness.

She becomes a loyal friend of Harry and an active member of Dumbledore's Army.

Although other students frequently ridicule her, Luna demonstrates considerable emotional insight and courage.

She is imprisoned by Death Eaters because of her father's opposition to Voldemort but is eventually rescued.

DOBBY AND KREACHER

Dobby is a house-elf who initially serves the Malfoy family.

Harry helps secure Dobby's freedom in Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets.

Dobby becomes a devoted ally and helps Harry escape imprisonment at Malfoy Manor.

Bellatrix Lestrange fatally wounds Dobby during the escape.

Kreacher is the house-elf of the Black family. He initially demonstrates prejudice and hostility toward Harry.

After Harry begins treating him with greater respect, Kreacher becomes more cooperative.

Kreacher's knowledge of Regulus Black helps Harry locate information about Slytherin's locket Horcrux.

THE ORDER OF THE PHOENIX

The Order of the Phoenix is a secret resistance organization founded by Dumbledore to oppose Voldemort.

Its members include witches and wizards from different professional and social backgrounds.

Important members include Sirius Black, Remus Lupin, Molly and Arthur Weasley, Kingsley Shacklebolt, Nymphadora Tonks, Alastor Moody, and Severus Snape.

The Order protects individuals targeted by Voldemort, gathers intelligence, and participates in military resistance.

DUMBLEDORE'S ARMY

Dumbledore's Army is a student organization founded after Dolores Umbridge refuses to provide meaningful practical instruction in Defense Against the Dark Arts.

Harry teaches members defensive spells and practical techniques.

Hermione helps organize the group, which meets secretly in the Room of Requirement.

During Voldemort's control of Hogwarts, the organization becomes a center of student resistance under leaders including Neville Longbottom.

THE DEATH EATERS

The Death Eaters are Voldemort's followers.

Many embrace pure-blood supremacy, although their motivations also include fear, ambition, ideological extremism, and personal loyalty.

Important Death Eaters include Bellatrix Lestrange, Lucius Malfoy, Barty Crouch Jr., and several members of other influential families.

The Death Eaters use torture, murder, intimidation, and political infiltration to establish Voldemort's power.

Bellatrix Lestrange is particularly devoted to Voldemort and murders several important characters, including Sirius Black and Dobby.

THE MINISTRY UNDER VOLDEMORT

The Ministry of Magic initially resists acknowledging Voldemort's return.

Following the assassination of Rufus Scrimgeour, Voldemort's supporters take control of the Ministry.

Pius Thicknesse becomes Minister for Magic while under the influence of the Imperius Curse.

The Ministry begins persecuting Muggle-born witches and wizards through the Muggle-Born Registration Commission.

The government uses propaganda and fabricated legal accusations to portray Harry Potter and other resistance members as criminals.

The takeover demonstrates how existing government institutions can be exploited by authoritarian movements.

THE BATTLE OF HOGWARTS

The Battle of Hogwarts is the final major confrontation of the Second Wizarding War.

Harry returns to the school to locate and destroy Rowena Ravenclaw's diadem, one of Voldemort's Horcruxes.

Hogwarts teachers, students, members of the Order, and other allies defend the castle.

Several important characters die, including Fred Weasley, Remus Lupin, Nymphadora Tonks, and Severus Snape.

Harry voluntarily enters the Forbidden Forest and allows Voldemort to strike him with the Killing Curse.

Harry survives, while the accidental Horcrux inside him is destroyed.

Neville kills Nagini, eliminating the final external Horcrux.

Harry later confronts Voldemort in the Great Hall. Voldemort's own Killing Curse rebounds, killing him.

The battle marks the collapse of Voldemort's regime and the end of the Second Wizarding War.

CANON NOTES AND LIMITATIONS

Character motivations are often revealed gradually, and early appearances may be intentionally misleading.

Snape's secret opposition to Voldemort does not mean that his behavior toward students was consistently justified or kind.

Draco Malfoy's hesitation to commit murder does not erase his earlier support for prejudiced beliefs.

This document follows the novels, including deaths and revelations that differ in presentation from the films.

The epilogue establishes several adult relationships and children, but it does not provide a complete account of every character's later career or life.

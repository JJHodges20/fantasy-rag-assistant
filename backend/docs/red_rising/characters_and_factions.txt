TITLE: Red Rising — Characters and Factions
SERIES: Red Rising
COVERAGE: Red Rising, Golden Son, Morning Star, Iron Gold, Dark Age, and Light Bringer
SPOILER LEVEL: FULL
CANON BASIS: Pierce Brown's published Red Rising novels through Light Bringer

OVERVIEW

Red Rising follows individuals whose relationships and loyalties change as the Society collapses and the struggle to establish a different civilization intensifies. Characters who initially appear to be friends become enemies, while former opponents sometimes develop mutual respect.

The series explores freedom, inherited privilege, revolutionary violence, political responsibility, betrayal, and the personal cost of war.

DARROW OF LYKOS

Darrow is born a Red in the mining settlement of Lykos on Mars. He works as a Helldiver, operating dangerous machinery to extract resources beneath the planet's surface.

His wife, Eo, dreams of a world where people are no longer divided into oppressive castes. Her execution by the Society becomes the turning point in Darrow's life.

Recruited by the Sons of Ares, Darrow undergoes an extraordinary physical transformation performed by the Violet Carver Mickey. He emerges with the appearance and abilities of a Gold.

Under a fabricated Gold identity, Darrow enters the Institute, where he learns to lead soldiers and manipulate the political structures of the ruling class.

Eventually known as the Reaper of Mars, Darrow becomes one of the revolution's most important military leaders.

His victories help destroy Octavia's government, but his later decisions create enormous casualties and political divisions. During the second part of the series, Darrow struggles with the consequences of becoming a symbol of war.

VIRGINIA AU AUGUSTUS — MUSTANG

Virginia, commonly called Mustang, is a brilliant Gold strategist and the daughter of Nero au Augustus.

She develops a close relationship with Darrow despite the dangers surrounding his hidden identity.

Unlike many members of the Gold aristocracy, Virginia believes the existing hierarchy is unjust and supports a more inclusive political order.

After Octavia's defeat, Virginia becomes Sovereign of the Solar Republic.

Her leadership is tested by hostile Gold factions, internal political unrest, and the difficulty of creating democratic institutions during an ongoing war.

Virginia and Darrow marry and have a son named Pax.

SEVRO AU BARCA AND THE HOWLERS

Sevro is an unconventional Gold warrior, known for his fierce loyalty, irreverent personality, and willingness to disregard aristocratic conventions.

He becomes one of Darrow's closest allies after their experiences at the Institute.

Sevro is the son of Fitchner au Barca, the original Ares, who secretly leads the Sons of Ares while operating within Gold society.

After Fitchner's death, Sevro helps carry the rebellion forward and becomes closely associated with the Howlers.

The Howlers are a combat unit and brotherhood whose members undertake dangerous missions, often using unconventional tactics.

Sevro eventually marries Victra au Julii, another Gold who becomes an important ally of the Rising.

CASSIUS AU BELLONA

Cassius comes from an influential Gold family and initially forms a friendship with Darrow at the Institute.

Their relationship deteriorates after Cassius discovers that Darrow killed his brother Julian during the Institute's entrance trials.

Cassius becomes a prominent opponent of Darrow, but his understanding of honor eventually challenges his loyalty to the Society.

In Morning Star, Cassius helps Darrow defeat Octavia au Lune.

He later lives in exile while protecting and educating Lysander au Lune, Octavia's grandson.

Cassius eventually returns to Darrow's side and becomes central to the effort to resist the renewed Gold offensive.

In Light Bringer, Cassius dies when Lysander shoots him after he refuses to permit the use of the biological weapon Eidmi. His death represents one of the series' most consequential betrayals.

LYSANDER AU LUNE

Lysander is the grandson of Octavia au Lune and an heir to the former ruling dynasty.

After Octavia's death, Cassius takes him into exile, hoping to teach him a more honorable understanding of responsibility.

As an adult, Lysander becomes convinced that Gold leadership can restore order to the solar system.

He increasingly pursues political authority through alliances, manipulation, and military conflict.

Lysander presents himself as a reformer while repeatedly choosing methods that preserve elite dominance.

His killing of Cassius and decision to keep Eidmi demonstrate how far he has moved from the ideals Cassius attempted to teach him.

ADRIUS AU AUGUSTUS — THE JACKAL

Adrius, known as the Jackal, is Virginia's twin brother.

He possesses exceptional intelligence but demonstrates extreme cruelty, ambition, and a desire for control.

Adrius manipulates Gold politics, betrays allies, and becomes one of Darrow's most dangerous adversaries.

He is responsible for terrible atrocities, including the use of nuclear weapons against civilian populations.

Following Octavia's downfall, Adrius is captured and publicly executed.

Later novels introduce a cloned version of Adrius, known as the Abomination, whose existence creates new threats. The clone is not simply the original Adrius returned to life.

FITCHNER, RAGNAR, AND SEFI

Fitchner is the original Ares and one of the central architects of the Sons of Ares. His position inside Gold society allows him to help organize revolutionary resistance.

Ragnar Volarus is an Obsidian warrior who becomes a close ally of Darrow. His courage and growing belief in freedom challenge Gold stereotypes about Obsidians.

Ragnar is mortally wounded by Aja au Grimmus in Morning Star.

His sister, Sefi the Quiet, becomes an important Obsidian leader, seeking independence and dignity for her people.

Sefi is later killed by Volsung Fá, whose campaign exploits Obsidian grievances and ambitions.

OTHER IMPORTANT CHARACTERS

Eo is Darrow's first wife, whose death inspires his resistance and whose dream of freedom becomes central to the revolution.

Roque au Fabii is a gifted Gold naval commander and former friend of Darrow. Unable to accept Darrow's betrayal of Gold society, he becomes his opponent and eventually dies by suicide after a decisive defeat.

Lyria is a Red refugee whose experiences reveal the hardships endured by ordinary people after the revolution. Her storyline demonstrates that political liberation does not immediately produce safety or prosperity.

Ephraim ti Horn is a former Gray soldier turned criminal whose involvement with Pax and other characters eventually leads him toward sacrifice and redemption.

Diomedes au Raa is a Rim Gold whose sense of honor and concern for his people make him an important potential ally in the struggle against the Core's destructive ambitions.

MAJOR FACTIONS

The Sons of Ares are the revolutionary network responsible for organizing resistance and helping Darrow infiltrate Gold society.

The Solar Republic is the post-revolutionary government that attempts to replace inherited caste rule with representative institutions.

The Society Remnant consists of Gold loyalists and military powers seeking to defeat the Republic and restore Gold supremacy.

The Howlers are Darrow and Sevro's closely connected special-operations allies.

The Rim Dominion represents powerful outer-system interests, especially the Gold families governing the moons of Jupiter and surrounding territories.

The Daughters of Athena are a revolutionary organization active in the Rim, challenging oppressive political structures there.

The Volk are a major Obsidian force whose leadership and loyalties change throughout the later novels.

CANON NOTES AND LIMITATIONS

Character alignments change significantly between the original trilogy and the later novels.

Some characters die permanently, while others are replaced, impersonated, or genetically recreated. Such distinctions matter when answering questions about identity.

The final political and personal outcomes of surviving characters remain unresolved at the end of Light Bringer.

TITLE: Mistborn — Characters and Factions
SERIES: Mistborn
COVERAGE: Mistborn Era One, Mistborn Era Two, and Mistborn: Secret History
SPOILER LEVEL: FULL
CANON BASIS: Brandon Sanderson's published Mistborn novels and relevant Cosmere fiction

OVERVIEW

Mistborn follows characters whose actions transform the political, religious, and magical history of Scadrial.

The first era centers on Vin, Kelsier, Elend Venture, and Sazed as they overthrow the Final Empire and confront the divine conflict between Ruin and Preservation.

The second era follows Waxillium Ladrian, Wayne, Marasi Colms, and Steris Harms as they investigate conspiracies and confront dangers that threaten their increasingly industrialized civilization.

Some individuals, including Kelsier, Marsh, and Harmony, connect the two historical periods.

ERA ONE — VIN

Vin is a young skaa thief who grows up surviving through suspicion, deception, and cautious relationships.

She possesses extraordinary Allomantic abilities and is recruited by Kelsier after he recognizes her potential.

Under the guidance of Kelsier and his crew, Vin develops into a skilled Mistborn and learns to trust others.

She infiltrates the nobility while posing as Valette Renoux, allowing her to gather intelligence about the Final Empire.

During this assignment, she develops a relationship with Elend Venture.

Vin eventually discovers the weakness of the Lord Ruler's immortality and defeats him.

She later confronts Ruin, learns the true nature of Preservation, and becomes the temporary holder of Preservation's power.

In The Hero of Ages, Vin sacrifices herself to destroy Ruin's holder, Ati, allowing Sazed to take up both Shards.

VIN'S HEMALURGIC EARRING

An important revelation concerns Vin's earring, which she believes is a personal keepsake connected to her mother.

The earring is actually a Hemalurgic spike that grants additional power to her bronze Allomancy.

It also gives Ruin a means of influencing her perceptions and actions.

When Vin removes the earring, she can escape this particular connection to Ruin and access Preservation's power more fully.

This revelation helps explain why Vin was manipulated throughout much of the original trilogy.

KELSIER — THE SURVIVOR

Kelsier is a charismatic Mistborn known as the Survivor of Hathsin.

He survived imprisonment in the Pits of Hathsin, where his wife Mare died.

Kelsier recruits a talented crew to overthrow the Lord Ruler and inspire a skaa revolution.

He develops a close mentor relationship with Vin, encouraging her to use her abilities and trust the people around her.

Kelsier is publicly killed by the Lord Ruler, but his death becomes a powerful symbol of resistance.

The religion of Survivorism later develops around his memory.

Mistborn: Secret History reveals that Kelsier's consciousness persists after his physical death as a Cognitive Shadow.

He remains involved in the conflict between Preservation and Ruin and ultimately survives beyond the Catacendre.

During Era Two, Kelsier is revealed to be the Sovereign who helped the people of Southern Scadrial survive.

He also becomes associated with the Ghostbloods, a secret organization concerned with protecting Scadrial and acquiring knowledge and power.

ELEND VENTURE

Elend is a nobleman who questions the Final Empire's oppressive political system.

He supports philosophical ideas about freedom and representative government and eventually becomes romantically involved with Vin.

Following the Lord Ruler's defeat, Elend attempts to govern Luthadel under a new political system.

His idealism is repeatedly challenged by warfare, betrayal, and the practical difficulties of leadership.

After consuming lerasium near the Well of Ascension, Elend becomes an exceptionally powerful Mistborn.

He later serves as emperor during the struggle against Ruin.

During the final battles of The Hero of Ages, Elend dies fighting Marsh and Ruin's forces.

His sacrifice helps prevent Ruin from recovering the power contained in the hidden atium supply.

SAZED — THE HERO OF AGES

Sazed is a Terris Keeper and full Feruchemist.

He stores historical records, religious teachings, and other information inside metalminds, particularly copperminds.

Sazed becomes an important mentor and ally to Vin, Elend, and Kelsier's crew.

He is deeply interested in recovering the religious traditions that the Lord Ruler attempted to destroy.

His relationship with Tindwyl, another Terris Keeper, significantly influences his emotional development.

After Tindwyl's death, Sazed experiences a crisis of faith and struggles to find meaning in the religious traditions he has preserved.

At the end of The Hero of Ages, Sazed realizes that he is the true Hero of Ages described by the prophecies.

He takes up the powers of both Preservation and Ruin, becoming Harmony.

As Harmony, Sazed rebuilds Scadrial and attempts to guide its inhabitants without eliminating their freedom.

In Era Two, he struggles to act because Preservation and Ruin place competing demands upon him.

RASHEK — THE LORD RULER

Rashek is a Terris man who kills Alendi and takes the power of the Well of Ascension.

He becomes the Lord Ruler and establishes the Final Empire.

His government relies on oppression, religious control, and supernatural enforcement.

Rashek possesses extraordinarily powerful Allomancy and Feruchemy, including the ability to Compound attributes.

His ability to preserve his youth allows him to rule for approximately one thousand years.

Despite his cruelty, he also makes preparations intended to protect humanity from Ruin's eventual return.

Vin eventually kills him by disrupting the metalminds responsible for sustaining his youth.

MARSH AND THE STEEL INQUISITORS

Marsh is Kelsier's older brother and originally works to help the rebellion.

He infiltrates the Steel Ministry but is transformed into a Steel Inquisitor through Hemalurgy.

Marsh survives the collapse of the Final Empire and becomes increasingly subject to Ruin's influence.

During the final conflict, he kills Elend Venture.

However, Marsh also contributes to Ruin's defeat by acting against Ruin's control at a crucial moment.

Marsh survives the Catacendre and remains alive during Era Two.

He becomes associated with legends of death and is sometimes called Ironeyes.

KELSIER'S CREW

Kelsier's original crew includes several important figures who contribute different skills to the rebellion.

Dockson helps organize the crew's operations and logistics.

Breeze is a skilled emotional Allomancer who uses brass to influence others.

Hammond, commonly called Ham, is a Pewterarm who combines physical strength with an interest in philosophy.

Clubs is a Smoker who creates copperclouds and assists with hiding Allomantic activity.

Spook is a Tineye whose loyalty and experiences become particularly important in The Hero of Ages.

After the Catacendre, Spook becomes the Lord Mistborn and helps lead the rebuilding of society.

TENSOON AND THE KANDRA

TenSoon is a kandra who initially operates as a servant and infiltrator.

He impersonates OreSeur, another kandra assigned to Vin, and develops a meaningful relationship with her.

TenSoon's actions challenge the expectations of obedience imposed upon kandra society.

His eventual opposition to Ruin helps demonstrate that the kandra possess independent moral judgment.

During Era Two, the kandra continue to serve Harmony and undertake important missions.

Other significant kandra include MeLaan, who assists Wax and Wayne.

ERA TWO — WAXILLIUM LADRIAN

Waxillium Ladrian, usually called Wax, is a nobleman and former lawman who spent much of his adult life in the Roughs.

He is a Twinborn known as a Crasher, combining steel Allomancy with iron Feruchemy.

Wax can Push on nearby metal objects and change his own physical weight.

These powers make him exceptionally effective in combat, especially when using firearms.

After returning to Elendel, Wax assumes responsibility for his noble house and becomes involved in investigations concerning the Set.

He develops a complicated relationship with Harmony, who repeatedly involves him in events that threaten Scadrial.

Wax eventually marries Steris Harms and becomes an important political leader.

During The Lost Metal, Wax helps prevent the destruction of Elendel and discovers new possibilities involving Allomancy.

WAYNE

Wayne is Wax's closest friend and investigative partner.

He possesses bendalloy Allomancy, allowing him to create areas where time passes more quickly relative to the outside world.

He also uses gold Feruchemy to store health and recover from injuries.

Wayne is known for his disguises, unusual trading habits, humor, and unconventional methods.

Despite his playful behavior, he carries profound guilt over having killed an innocent man earlier in his life.

His friendship with Wax becomes one of the emotional foundations of Era Two.

In The Lost Metal, Wayne sacrifices himself to stop a devastating explosion that would otherwise destroy Elendel.

His death represents the culmination of his growth from a guilt-ridden criminal into a genuinely selfless hero.

MARASI COLMS

Marasi is an intelligent investigator and the younger half-sister of Steris Harms.

She possesses cadmium Allomancy, allowing her to create a bubble in which time passes more slowly relative to the outside world.

Marasi initially admires Wax and becomes involved in his investigations.

Over time, she establishes her own professional identity as a member of law enforcement.

Her intelligence and commitment to public service make her increasingly important in confronting the Set.

In The Lost Metal, Marasi works with members of the Ghostbloods but ultimately chooses not to join their secretive organization.

STERIS HARMS

Steris is a noblewoman whose meticulous planning and attention to detail initially cause others to underestimate her.

She becomes engaged to Wax through a practical marriage arrangement, but their relationship develops into genuine love and mutual respect.

Steris is known for preparing detailed contingency plans for unexpected situations.

Her organizational skills, understanding of legal systems, and financial knowledge prove essential during several crises.

She becomes an important political and administrative partner to Wax.

In The Lost Metal, her planning helps organize responses to the threat against Elendel.

PAALM — LESSIE AND BLEEDER

Paalm is a kandra who operates under several identities.

As Lessie, she works alongside Wax in the Roughs and becomes his romantic partner.

After her apparent death, Wax discovers that Lessie was actually a kandra assigned to him by Harmony.

Paalm later adopts the identity Bleeder and attempts to break free from Harmony's influence.

Her actions cause widespread unrest and force Wax to confront difficult questions about divine manipulation and personal freedom.

Her death in Shadows of Self deeply affects Wax and his relationship with Harmony.

THE SET AND AUTONOMY

The Set is a secretive criminal and political organization involved in kidnapping, technological experimentation, and attempts to control Scadrial's government.

Edwarn Ladrian, Wax's uncle, becomes an important figure within the organization.

Telsin Ladrian, Wax's sister, later emerges as another major leader connected to its plans.

The organization is associated with the influence of Autonomy, a Shard from outside Scadrial.

Autonomy seeks to extend its influence over Scadrial and challenges Harmony's ability to protect the planet.

The Set's activities culminate in a large-scale threat during The Lost Metal.

THE GHOSTBLOODS

The Ghostbloods are a secret organization led by Kelsier, who is known within the group as Thaidakar.

Their Scadrian operations are concerned with defending the planet against outside threats and acquiring knowledge of Investiture.

Members include individuals with powers and origins beyond Scadrial.

The Ghostbloods demonstrate that the events of Mistborn connect to a much larger Cosmere.

Although some of their goals overlap with those of the protagonists, their secrecy and methods create ethical tensions.

CANON NOTES AND LIMITATIONS

Several Era One characters survive into Era Two through supernatural means, rather than ordinary human longevity.

Kelsier's continued existence as a Cognitive Shadow should not be confused with a normal resurrection.

Marsh's survival depends upon extraordinary magical mechanisms and should not be treated as typical human aging.

The name Sovereign can refer to different political or historical roles. In Southern Scadrial, the Sovereign associated with the Bands of Mourning is Kelsier, not Rashek.

The final outcomes described here are those established by the end of The Lost Metal. Future Mistborn stories may expand the political, magical, and personal consequences of these events.
